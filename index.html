The approach

Three things we practise, every single class.

01

Connection

Weight, breath and intention, not memorised steps. You'll learn to send clear invitations, receive honest answers and stay with a partner through the in-between moments.

02

Musicality

Hear the layers: bass, melody, silence. We train your ear so the music tells you what to do next, because fusion means dancing blues, tango, zouk, contact and everything between to whatever song comes on.

03

Play

Mistakes are material. Games, constraints and swaps keep the room light, so you stop performing and start discovering what your body already knows.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need a partner or any experience?

Neither. We rotate partners throughout class, and everything is taught from the ground up. Many students arrive alone and leave with a new dance family.

What should I wear?

Anything you can move in. Shoes that let you pivot (suede soles, socks or smooth trainers) work best. Skip heavy grippy soles.

Do I have to lead or follow?

You choose, and you can switch whenever you like. Roles are about information flow, not gender, and everyone is encouraged to try both.

How do you approach consent and comfort?

Every class starts with simple agreements: you can say no, take a break or change partners at any time, no explanation needed. Touch is always opt-in.

Can you teach at our festival or studio?

Yes, I love it. Send dates, a rough headcount and your community's vibe, and I'll propose a workshop plan within two working days.
